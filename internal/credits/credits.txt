# Who made Glider PRO.
#
# Transcribed from GliderPRO/README.md lines 2 and 7-15, which is the whole of what the
# source release says about authorship: the game has no credits screen, its About box is
# three pictures, and a house file has no author field. So this cannot be read out of the
# original at runtime and has to be carried as data.
#
# It is a data file rather than a Go literal so that it can be checked against its source.
# credits_test.go reads the vendored README and fails if a person named here is not named
# there, or if a house named there is not named here -- which is the only way a file like
# this stays true after somebody edits it.
#
# Syntax: `[Section]`, then `who | what`, then any line with no `|` as a note. Lines are
# not wrapped when drawn, and the shell's test fails one too wide for the screen.

[the game]
John Calhoun | wrote Glider PRO
Casady & Greene Inc. | published it, 1994

[the houses]
John Calhoun, Kim Money | Demo House, CD Demo House
Jonathan Chin, John Calhoun | Davis Station, Metropolis, Titanic
Jonathan Chin | Grand Prix, Leviathan, ImagineHouse PRO II, In The Mirror
Ward Hartenstein | Land of Illusion, Nemo's Market, Rainbow's End, SpacePods
John Calhoun, Jonathan Chin, Steve Sullivan, Ward Hartenstein | Slumberland
Shawn Brenneman | Teddy World
Steve Sullivan | The Asylum Pro
Jonathan Chin signs his houses as Paul Finn, which is the name their own banners carry.
Six houses are credited to nobody: Art Museum, California or Bust!, Castle o' the Air,
Empty House, Fun House and Sampler. The third of those names John Calhoun in its banner,
and Sampler -- saved in 2000, five years after the rest -- names only "Omid".

[the illustrations]
John R. Neill | the Ozma plate (PICT 3975), from "Ozma of Oz"
Winsor McCay | the About box (PICT 153), from a "Little Nemo in Slumberland" strip

[this port]
gliderGo | a Go port of the GPL v2 source, 2026
It ships all of the art, the sounds and the houses above, decoded from the released
source by `make assets` and committed beside it. None of that work is ours.
